The Atelier

A perfectly crafted journey begins not with a destination, but with a conversation. Here are the people to call when you’re ready to pen the story of your next great adventure.

Aidan Conway

Founder & Lead Advisor

Before he ever booked a honeymoon or chartered a yacht, Aidan Conway spent years as a critical care nurse — navigating high-stakes environments where precision isn't a preference, it's the baseline. That discipline never left him. He simply transposed it onto a different kind of care.

Conway founded Conway Travel Collective on the belief that the most extraordinary travel experiences aren't discovered — they're designed. Each commission begins with the same question he asked in every clinical intake: What does this person actually need?

His clientele are people who give their full attention to others all day. Conway gives his full attention back.
