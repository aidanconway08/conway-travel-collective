Our Story

Global Reach.

There’s a particular kind of precision that comes from working in critical care. Aidan Conway knows it well — and it’s exactly why he built Conway Travel Collective.

As a critical care nurse, Conway spent years making high-stakes decisions where overlooked details carry real consequences. He has simply transposed that framework onto a different kind of care — designing experiences so seamless that the client never once has to think about logistics.

“Travel should be completely stress-free. Someone else manages every detail. You just show up.”

For those who spend their days managing complexity on behalf of others — whether in a boardroom, a creative studio, or anywhere life takes them — the promise of genuine surrender lands differently. The appeal of handing every detail to someone who cares as much as you do, and simply arriving, is not trivial. For many of our clients, it is something close to radical.

Conway Travel Collective brings the same commitment to craft and relationship that defines boutique culture — and applies it to the world’s most extraordinary destinations.

The Clinical Case for Bespoke Travel

The analogy Conway draws between nursing and travel planning isn’t a branding exercise. It’s operational philosophy. In critical care, the stakes of overlooked details are measured in outcomes. Conway has simply transposed that framework onto a different kind of care — the art of designing an experience so seamless that the client never once has to think about logistics.

For those who spend their days managing complexity — executives, creatives, entrepreneurs, and anyone who simply values their time — the promise of genuine surrender is not trivial. To arrive at a private villa on a coastline they’ve dreamed about, or an overwater bungalow where the only decision is whether to order breakfast before or after the sunrise, having had none of it require a single stressful moment — that is the product.

Access as the Product

Conway Travel Collective operates in the tier of travel where the experience itself is the point of differentiation — not the destination. The inventory Conway works with doesn’t live on booking platforms. Private villas. Ultra-luxury resort relationships. Bespoke yacht charters.

The agency is also developing proprietary technology: a custom CRM, AI automation tools, and a unified communications platform designed for the operational demands of high-touch travel planning — an uncommonly ambitious infrastructure investment for a boutique firm.
